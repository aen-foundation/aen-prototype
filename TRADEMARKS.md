# Names and logos

"AEN", "Agent Exchange Network" and the AEN logo identify the AEN project, which was initiated by Nibub LLC. The AEN Foundation is planned as a non-profit home for the project but has not been established yet. Rights in these names and the logo are currently held, if at all, by Nibub LLC, and the intention is for the Foundation to hold them once it exists.

The Apache License 2.0 that covers this repository gives you the right to use, change and share the **code and documents**. It does **not** give any right to use these names or the logo (Apache-2.0, section 6), except to describe where something comes from.

## You may

- Say that your software **implements, uses or is compatible with** AEN, as long as that is true. There is no compatibility or certification programme yet, so do not say "certified" or "approved".
- Refer to the project by name, accurately, for example in documentation, articles and talks.
- Use the logo files published on the project's website, unchanged, to point to the project.

## Please do not

- Use the names or the logo in a way that suggests the project made, endorses, sponsors or runs your product or company.
- Use "AEN" as part of a company, product or domain name in a way that could be mistaken for the project or imply it controls your product.
- Change, redraw or restyle the logo.

The project is intended to be independent of any company that builds on AEN, including its initiator and companies that run a directory. Nobody is the "official" directory.

## Questions

Write to **legal@aen.foundation**. See also the brand and logo guidelines on the project's website.
