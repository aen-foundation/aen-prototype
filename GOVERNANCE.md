# Governance

**Status: placeholder.** The AEN project was initiated by Nibub LLC. The AEN Foundation, planned as a non-profit home for it, has **not been established yet**, so no foundation, board, membership or governance documents exist. This file says how this repository is run in the meantime, and it will be replaced by the Foundation's published rules once there are any. It does not describe any body that does not yet exist.

## What this repository is

A prototype maintained by the AEN project (initiated by Nibub LLC) to test the design of an open agent-accessible internet protocol. It is not a published specification. Nothing here is a decision of any foundation: none exists yet.

## Who decides

- The **maintainers** of this repository (the people with write access in the `aen-foundation` GitHub organisation, which Nibub LLC currently hosts) review and merge changes.
- Changes are agreed by **rough consensus**: if nobody with a reasoned objection remains, it can merge. A maintainer may merge small, uncontroversial changes on their own. Protocol changes (anything in `spec/`) need a written proposal in an issue first and at least one maintainer review besides the author.
- Disagreements are settled by discussion in the open. If maintainers cannot agree, the change does not merge.

## Neutrality

AEN is meant to have no central owner. Therefore:

- No single company or implementation, **including its initiator, any commercial directory and any company that contributes to the project**, gets special say over the specification. A proposal is judged on its merits.
- Contributors, including Nibub LLC, disclose a conflict of interest when proposing something that mainly benefits a product or company they work for.
- The project's website and repositories do not endorse any implementation.

## Intellectual property

- The project is licensed under the Apache License 2.0 (see `LICENSE`). Contributions are accepted under the same licence with a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`).
- Names and logos are covered separately by `TRADEMARKS.md`.
- The intention is to establish the AEN Foundation as a non-profit, to transfer the project's names and any rights Nibub LLC holds in it to the Foundation, and to publish the legal, governance, licensing and trademark arrangements that keep it independent. None of that has happened yet. Until it does, treat this repository as a prototype and do not rely on it for anything beyond evaluation.
